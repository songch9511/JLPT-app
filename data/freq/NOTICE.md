# data/freq — sources and licence

The frequency scores (`n{l}.json`), their method codes (`n{l}.src.json`) and the introduction orders
(`order-n{l}.json`) are generated by `scripts/enrich/freq.py` and are **derived from**:

- **wordfreq 3.1.1** by Robyn Speer, Japanese "best" word list —
  https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq — data licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). wordfreq contains data from OPUS
  OpenSubtitles 2018 (http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php; data originating from
  http://www.opensubtitles.org/), Wikipedia, web text (OSCAR), Twitter, Reddit and other sources
  listed in its README; tokenised with MeCab (Kudo 2005) and IPAdic.
- **JMdict** (Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group) — priority tags, "usually written
  in kana" flags, part of speech and alternative spellings, via `jamdict-data` 1.5 (compiled 2021-04-17).
  https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project ·
  licence: https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike).

bundles data built from them (for example `public/data/n*.json` with the extra columns), must credit
wordfreq (and its sources, including OpenSubtitles) and the EDRDG, and keep derived data under CC BY-SA.
The app's code is not affected. See docs/plan/08-word-data.md §1.
